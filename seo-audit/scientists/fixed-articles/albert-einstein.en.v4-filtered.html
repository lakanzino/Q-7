Childhood in Ulm and Munich: Compass and Violin

Albert Einstein was born 14 March 1879 in Ulm, Württemberg, Germany. [Britannica] [Nobel Facts] His father Hermann Einstein was a featherbed salesman and later electrochemical factory owner. [Britannica] His mother Pauline Koch ran the household. [Britannica] The family was secular middle-class Jewish and did not observe religious rites. [Britannica] He had one sister Maja born two years later. [Britannica]

At age 5 he received a compass and wondered about invisible force. [Britannica] At 12 he read a geometry book he called holy geometry book. [Britannica] He played violin and loved Mozart lifelong. [Britannica] At Luitpold Gymnasium he felt stifled by Prussian education. [Britannica] A teacher told him he would never amount to anything. [Britannica]

In 1894 his father's business failed and family moved to Milan. [Britannica] He was left in Munich boardinghouse and ran away after six months. [Britannica] In 1896 he renounced German citizenship and became stateless. [Britannica] In 1901 he became Swiss citizen. [Britannica]

From Zurich to Bern: Failed Student, Genius Clerk

In 1895 he failed ETH entrance but with high math scores entered Aarau school. [Britannica] He graduated in 1896 and entered ETH Zurich. [Britannica] In 1900 he graduated as teacher of physics and mathematics. [Britannica] His teacher Hermann Minkowski later geometrized relativity. [Stanford] In 1905 he received doctorate from University of Zurich with thesis on molecular dimensions. [Britannica]

In 1902 he became expert class III at Swiss patent office Bern. [Britannica] He evaluated electromagnetic inventions. [Nobel Bio] He spent free time on theoretical physics. [Nobel Bio] He befriended Michele Besso lifelong. [Stanford] In 1909 he became professor at University of Zurich. [Britannica] In 1911 Prague, 1912 ETH, 1913 director of Kaiser Wilhelm Institute Berlin. [Britannica]

Annus Mirabilis 1905

1905 is called Einstein's miracle year. [Britannica] He published four papers in Annalen der Physik. [Britannica] All four changed 20th century physics. [APS 2025] He was 26 and a patent clerk. [Nobel Bio]

Four Blows that Shook Physics

1. Photoelectric Effect — Light is Quantized — March 1905

Photoelectric paper received 17 March 1905 and published 9 June 1905. [Einstein 1905a DOI] Title was heuristic viewpoint on production and transformation of light. [Einstein 1905a] Einstein said light consists of quanta with energy E=hν. [Nobel Facts] This explained why threshold frequency is needed not intensity. [Nobel Facts] Millikan verified it precisely in 1916. [Nobel Speech 1922] It brought Nobel 1921 awarded 1922. [Nobel 1921] It is basis for solar cells and smartphone cameras. [Nobel Stories]

2. Brownian Motion — Atoms are Real — May 1905

Brownian paper published 11 May 1905. [Britannica] Einstein explained random pollen motion by molecular collisions. [Britannica] His formula made Avogadro number measurable. [Stanford] Jean Perrin confirmed it 1908 and won Nobel 1926. [Britannica] It ended doubt about atoms. [Stanford]

3. Special Relativity and E=mc² — June & September 1905

Special relativity paper 30 June 1905 titled On the Electrodynamics of Moving Bodies. [Einstein 1905c DOI] Two postulates: laws same in all inertial frames and light speed constant. [Britannica] It led to time dilation and length contraction. [Britannica] Fourth paper September 1905 gave E=mc² mass-energy equivalence. [Britannica] It later explained nuclear energy. [Britannica]

4. General Relativity — Gravity is Curved Spacetime — November 1915

Einstein completed field equations November 1915. [Britannica] Theory says gravity is curvature of spacetime not force. [Britannica] It predicted light bending near Sun 1.75 arcseconds. [Britannica] Eddington confirmed it at eclipse 29 May 1919. [Britannica] News in Times London 7 Nov 1919 headlined revolution. [Britannica] It made him world famous. [Britannica]

Personal Life, Beliefs, Ethics

Einstein married Mileva Maric 1903 fellow student from Serbia. [Britannica] Children Lieserl 1902, Hans Albert 1904, Eduard 1910. [Britannica] Marriage ended divorce 1919. [Britannica] Same year married cousin Elsa Lowenthal. [Britannica] Elsa died 1936. [Britannica]

Known for humor, humility, messy hair. [Britannica] Played violin daily. [Britannica] Friend of Charlie Chaplin. [Britannica] Spoke English with German accent. [Britannica]

Faith and Doubt: Spinoza's God

Einstein grew up in secular Jewish family. [Britannica] Had brief religious phase at 12. [Britannica] Later said I believe in Spinoza's God who reveals himself in harmony of world. [Stanford] Letter 3 Jan 1954 to Eric Gutkind said word God is expression of human weakness. [Stanford] Called himself agnostic not militant atheist. [Stanford] Said science without religion is lame, religion without science blind. [Stanford]

Between Duty and Conscience: Pacifism to Roosevelt Letter

Einstein was pacifist and signed anti-war manifesto 1914. [Britannica] In 1933 when Hitler came to power he was in US. [Britannica] Nazis burned his books and called Jewish physics. [Britannica] He resigned from Prussian Academy. [Britannica] In 1933 moved to Institute for Advanced Study Princeton. [Nobel Bio] Became US citizen 1940 but kept Swiss. [Britannica]

On 2 Aug 1939 signed letter to Roosevelt written by Leo Szilard. [Britannica] Warned Germany may build atomic bomb. [Britannica] It led to Manhattan Project. [Britannica] He did not work on project. [Britannica] Later said one great mistake was signing that letter. [Stanford] After war advocated nuclear disarmament and civil rights. [Britannica] In 1952 declined presidency of Israel. [Britannica]

On 18 April 1955 died of ruptured abdominal aortic aneurysm in Princeton. [Britannica] [Britannica Brain] He refused surgery saying I want to go when I want. [Stanford] He worked on unified field theory until hours before death. [Britannica]

FAQ About Albert Einstein

Who is Albert Einstein?
German-Swiss-American physicist who in 1905 gave photoelectric effect and special relativity and 1915 general relativity, Nobel 1921. [Nobel]
When did Einstein introduce relativity?
Special relativity 30 June 1905 and general relativity 25 Nov 1915. [Einstein 1905] [Britannica]
Why did Einstein get Nobel?
For discovery of law of photoelectric effect E=hν, not relativity. [Nobel 1921]
Was Einstein religious?
Secular Jewish agnostic Spinozist, 1954 Gutkind letter says God is expression of human weakness. [Stanford]
Did Einstein build atomic bomb?
No, did not work on Manhattan, only signed warning letter to Roosevelt 1939. [Britannica]
What is legacy today?
GPS uses relativity, solar cell and camera use photoelectric, LIGO uses general relativity. [Nobel Stories]
