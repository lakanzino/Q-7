Childhood in Vienna: Oilcloth Factory and Botany

Erwin Schrödinger was born 12 Aug 1887 in Erdberg Vienna. [Britannica] [Univie] Father Rudolf was oilcloth producer and botanist. [Britannica] Mother Georgine Bauer daughter of chemistry professor at TU Wien. [Britannica] He was only child. [Britannica] Father Catholic mother Lutheran. [Britannica] Maternal grandmother British, learned English at home. [Britannica]

At Akademisches Gymnasium Vienna top student. [Britannica] From 1906 to 1910 studied under Franz Exner and Friedrich Hasenöhrl at University of Vienna. [Britannica] PhD 1910. [Britannica] Assistant to Exner 1911. [Britannica] Read Schopenhauer deeply, interested in color theory and Vedanta. [Britannica]

From 1914 to 1918 served as artillery officer in Austrian fortress artillery Gorizia. [Britannica]

From Stuttgart to Zurich: Late Bloomer Professor

In 1920 assistant to Max Wien in Jena. [Britannica] Sept 1920 associate professor Stuttgart. [Britannica] 1921 full professor Breslau now Wroclaw. [Britannica] 1921-1927 professor Zurich. [Britannica Summary] There at 39 remarkably late age for theorist he produced foundations of wave mechanics. [Britannica] 1927 successor to Max Planck in Berlin. [Univie] 1933 resigned in protest of Jewish persecution and emigrated to Oxford. [Univie] [Britannica Summary]

Boiling Point: Winter 1925 in Arosa

Christmas 1925 spent in Arosa Switzerland. [Britannica] Inspired by de Broglie to treat electron as wave. [Britannica] Jan 1926 paper Quantisierung als Eigenwertproblem submitted. [Schrödinger 1926] It became basis of wave mechanics. [Britannica]

Three Blows that Shook Physics

1. Schrödinger Equation — 27 Jan 1926

First paper received 27 Jan 1926 published Annalen 384:361-376. [Schrödinger 1926 DOI] Equation iħ ∂ψ/∂t = Ĥ ψ describes evolution of wavefunction. [Britannica] Gave correct hydrogen energy levels. [Britannica] Second paper four weeks later solved harmonic oscillator, rigid rotor. [Britannica] Third May 1926 showed equivalence to Heisenberg matrix mechanics. [Britannica] Revolution in physics and chemistry. [Britannica]

2. Cat and Entanglement — 1935

In 1935 replied to Einstein EPR paper. [Britannica] Coined term Verschränkung entanglement. [Britannica] Thought experiment cat in box with quantum poison both alive and dead until observation. [Britannica] Aimed to show absurdity of Copenhagen at large scale. [Britannica] Today enduring popular symbol. [Britannica]

3. What Is Life? — 1944 Inspiring Molecular Biology

In 1940 at invitation of de Valera joined Dublin Institute for Advanced Studies. [Britannica Summary] Stayed 15 years. [Britannica Summary] 1944 book What Is Life? written. [Britannica Summary] Said gene stability explained by quantum physics. [Britannica Summary] Inspired Watson and Crick. [Britannica] 1956 retired and returned to Vienna. [Britannica Summary]
