Childhood in Kiel: A House of Law and Church

Max Planck was born 23 April 1858 in Kiel. [Britannica] [Nobel] His father was law professor. [Stanford] His grandfather was theology professor at Göttingen. [Stanford] Family values were duty, integrity, generosity. [Britannica]

From Munich to Berlin: Years That Disappointed Him

He entered Munich University fall 1874. [Britannica] Philipp von Jolly did not encourage him. [Britannica] He learned thermodynamics from Clausius self-study. [Britannica] In Berlin 1877-1878 he heard Helmholtz and Kirchhoff. [Britannica] He found lectures uninspiring. [Britannica] PhD July 1879 at 21. [Stanford] Thesis on second law. [Stanford] Professor at Kiel 1885. [Britannica] Professor Berlin 1889 succeeding Kirchhoff. [Britannica]

The Boiling Point: When a Formula Split Physics

Planck turned to blackbody radiation 1894. [Klein 1962] Rubens and Kurlbaum had precise data failing Wien at long waves. [Klein 1962] He learned results days before 19 Oct 1900 talk. [Klein 1962] On 19 Oct he presented interpolation formula. [Klein 1962] On 14 Dec 1900 he presented derivation with quanta. [Planck 1901] This is birth of quantum theory. [Nobel]

Three Blows That Shook Physics

1. Solving Ultraviolet Catastrophe — Dec 14 1900

Rayleigh-Jeans diverged at short waves. [Britannica UV] Planck assumed oscillator energy = n hν. [Planck 1901] h became new universal constant. [Britannica] Spectral density u = (8πhν³/c³)/(exp(hν/kT)-1). [Planck 1901] Matched experiments. [CODATA] He called it act of desperation. [Klein 1962] Einstein 1905 took quanta literally for photoelectric effect. [Einstein 1905]

2. Planck Constant h — Smallest Action

h = 6.62607015e-34 J·s exact since 2019 SI redefinition. [CODATA 2019] [Britannica] First estimate 6.55e-27 erg·s. [Klein 1962] Boltzmann constant k = 1.346e-16 erg/K also computed. [Klein 1962] Defined Planck natural units from h,c,G. [Stanford]

3. Champion of Relativity and Mentor

Early champion of special relativity 1905-1906. [Britannica] Mentored Heisenberg and Pauli Nobel laureates. [Britannica] Rector Berlin 1913-1914. [Britannica] President Kaiser Wilhelm Society 1930-1937. [Stanford]

Behind Equations: Love, Faith, and Repeated Loss

Faith and Doubt: From Church Elder to 1947 Letter

Raised Lutheran and church elder. [Enlightened] Role like assistant pastor. [Enlightened] Wrote 1937 “There can never be any real opposition between religion and science”. [Wikiquote] Letter 18 June 1947 “deeply religious but not personal Christian God”. [MPG Archive] Historians call deist. [Forum Phil]

Between Duty and Conscience: 1933–1945

Remained in Germany when many left. [Britannica] Met Hitler spring 1933 to plead for Jewish colleagues. [Cantor] Failed, Hitler furious. [Cantor] Defended Einstein and Haber. [Britannica] Resigned 1937 under pressure. [Britannica] House destroyed 1944 bombs. [Cantor] Library lost. [Cantor] Son Erwin in July 20 plot to assassinate Hitler.
